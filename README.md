SLOGEN Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard
📊 Project Overview
SLOGEN Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based Sales & Profitability Dashboard designed to analyze business performance. It provides insights into revenue, gross profit, profit margin, orders, units sold, countries, regions, sales channels, and product categories.
🎯 Objective
The main objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful visual insights that help businesses track performance, identify trends, compare markets, and make data-driven decisions.

📈 Dashboard Features
1. Monthly Revenue & Profit
Shows the monthly trend of Total Revenue and Total Gross Profit, making it easy to identify high- and low-performing months.
2. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
Compares the top-performing countries based on total revenue.
3. Revenue Share by Channel
Analyzes revenue contribution from different sales channels: Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, and Retail Partners.
4. Region-wise Comparison
Compares revenue performance across Central Europe, Northern Europe, Southern Europe, and Western Europe.
5. Country-wise Map
Provides a geographical view of revenue performance across European countries.
6. Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard by Product Category, Country, and Quarter (Q1–Q4).
